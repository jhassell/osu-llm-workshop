Read 02_data/datasets/penguins/DICTIONARY.md, then investigate 02_data/datasets/penguins/penguins_raw.csv before analyzing it.

One row is one adult penguin, measured at its nest in one breeding season.

Find every problem you can: in its structure, its units, its values, and whether the measurements behaved as described. For each problem, show the code you ran and what it printed, the rows affected, and your decision (fix, flag, or leave alone) with a reason. Write results/findings.md. Do not change the raw file and do not drop rows silently.

Then, treating the data as you decided, make the three plots that best show bill (culmen) depth in millimetres against bill (culmen) length in millimetres: do birds with longer bills have deeper bills?. Something other than that comparison could explain a difference: species. The species differ in bill length and bill depth at once, so pooling them can produce a relationship that holds for none of them. Show whether it does. Save the plots in results/figures/ and write results/report.md. Every number must come from the script's printed output.
