Changes were made. This file is an excerpt assembled for this workshop: 19 of the 30 data points, sampled every 5 minutes instead of every minute, two weeks only, rounded to the precision a building automation system would log, and dated 2017 rather than the source's 2018 (the simulation runs its weekly schedule on a calendar that starts on a Sunday, as 2017 does, so the 2017 dates put the occupied days on Monday to Saturday as documented). It was also assembled in ways that are part of the exercise; those are described in the instructor's notes and shown at the end of the stop.
