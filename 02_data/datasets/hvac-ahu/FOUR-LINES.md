# The four lines, written before the first prompt

Observational unit: one row is one 5-minute reading from one air handling unit.

Outcome: supply air temperature (SA_TEMP) against its setpoint, in degrees Fahrenheit, while the supply fan runs.

Comparison: day against day across the two weeks. Does any day behave unlike the days around it, and is the equipment doing what it is told?

One plausible confounder: outdoor air temperature. On mild days the economizer cools with outdoor air and mechanical cooling is locked out, so supply air is not held at 55 F and a day can look odd only because of the weather. Compare days with similar outdoor temperatures before blaming the equipment.
