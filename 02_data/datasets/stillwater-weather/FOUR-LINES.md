# The four lines, written before the first prompt

Observational unit: one row is one calendar day at Stillwater Regional Airport.

Outcome: the day's maximum temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit.

Comparison: days with measurable rain (0.01 inch or more) against days without (dry or trace only).

One plausible confounder: season. Rain is more frequent in some months than others, and the months differ in temperature, so a raw wet-against-dry comparison mixes the effect of rain with the effect of the calendar.

What the reference got: the raw gap is smaller than the same-month gap, because rain falls most often in warm months. See reference/report.md and reference/extension.md.
