## The data

1824 rows, one per day, from 2021-01-01 to 2025-12-31 at STILLWATER REGIONAL AIRPORT, OK US. The calendar
has 1826 days, so 2 dates are absent from the file entirely
(2025-07-11, 2025-07-12). Blank values: TMAX 1,
PRCP 8. Days used: 1815; dropped: 9 (blank high temperature
or precipitation). 178 days record a trace of precipitation as 0.00 with flag T;
they count as dry here. Snowfall and snow depth are blank on most days and are not used.
Extremes: -17 F on 2021-02-16 and 112 F on 2022-07-19.

## What the plots show

Days with measurable rain (0.01 inch or more, 377 days) had a mean high of
72.8 F against 76.0 F on the other 1438 days, a gap of -3.2 F
(figure 1). Rain is not spread evenly over the year: May had rain on
36% of days and September on
9%. Comparing wet and dry days within the same month
(figure 2) gives a weighted gap of -5.6 F. The same-month gap is larger than the raw one: rain falls most often in the warm spring and early summer months, which lifts the average high of wet days and hides part of the cooling. Season is a confounder here, and ignoring it understates the effect.
