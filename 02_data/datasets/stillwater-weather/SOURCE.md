Why this station: it is the first-order (ASOS) station in Stillwater with temperature, precipitation and wind for the whole period. The cooperative station STILLWATER 2 W (USC00348501, record from 1893) stopped reporting temperature in 2010, and the Oklahoma Mesonet site (USC00348499) has no wind in GHCN-Daily.
