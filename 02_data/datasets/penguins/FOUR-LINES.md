# The four lines, written before the first prompt

Observational unit: one row is one adult penguin, measured at its nest in one breeding season.

Outcome: bill (culmen) depth, in millimetres.

Comparison: bill (culmen) length, in millimetres: do birds with longer bills have deeper bills?

One plausible confounder: species. Species differ in both bill length and bill depth at once (Gentoo bills are long and shallow, Adelie bills short and deep), so pooling the three species can produce a relationship that holds for none of them.

What the reference got: pooled, the slope is negative; within each species it is positive. See reference/report.md and reference/extension.md.
