# Three open-access papers for Stop 3

Three real, peer-reviewed field studies on one question: do cover crops change how much carbon a cropland soil holds? They differ in place, design and what they measured, so a three-row evidence table (question, method, sample, finding) has something to compare. They are the fallback for anyone without three PDFs of their own in `mine/`.

All three are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0), which allows copying and redistribution with credit. Each license was checked on November 6, 2026 in three places: the publisher's article page, the license statement printed in the PDF, and the license URL in the DOI's Crossref record. Each DOI comes back "verified" from `03_lit/lit.py`, with no retraction found in Crossref as of that date. The PDFs are unchanged copies of the publishers' files; each has a text layer that pypdf extracts on every page.

The file names are the BibTeX keys `03_lit/lit.py` builds for these papers.

## gentsch2024cover.pdf

Gentsch, N., Riechers, F. L., Boy, J., Schweneker, D., Feuerstein, U., Heuermann, D., and Guggenberger, G. (2024). Cover crops improve soil structure and change organic carbon distribution in macroaggregate fractions. SOIL, 10(1), 139-150.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5194/soil-10-139-2024
- License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Statement on the publisher page: https://soil.copernicus.org/articles/10/139/2024/ ("This work is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License.")
- PDF from: https://soil.copernicus.org/articles/10/139/2024/soil-10-139-2024.pdf
- 12 pages. Long-term field trial at Asendorf, Germany.

## chahal2020cumulative.pdf

Chahal, I., Vyn, R. J., Mayers, D., and Van Eerd, L. L. (2020). Cumulative impact of cover crops on soil carbon sequestration and profitability in a temperate humid climate. Scientific Reports, 10, 13381.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-70224-6
- License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Statement on the publisher page, under "Rights and permissions": https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-70224-6
- PDF from: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-70224-6.pdf
- 11 pages. Long-term field experiment in Ontario, Canada, with an economic analysis.

## zheng2020cover.pdf

Zheng, H., Zhou, L., Wei, J., Tang, Q., Zou, Y., Tang, J., and Xu, H. (2020). Cover crops and chicken grazing in a winter fallow field improve soil carbon and nitrogen contents and decrease methane emissions. Scientific Reports, 10, 12607.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-69407-y
- License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Statement on the publisher page, under "Rights and permissions": https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-69407-y
- PDF from: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-69407-y.pdf
- 11 pages. Field experiment in a winter fallow rice field in Hunan Province, China.

## Using them

Credit the authors as above whenever you share a table, excerpt or figure built from these papers. The license covers the papers; it says nothing about whether a summary of them is right. That is what the evidence check in Stop 3 is for.
