# Where the shipped material comes from

Every dataset, paper and chart shipped for the stops, with its license. Each folder's own SOURCE.md or SOURCES.md has the full citation and the license statement as quoted from the source.

## Stop 2 datasets (02_data/datasets/)

- penguins/penguins_raw.csv: palmerpenguins (Horst, Hill and Gorman 2020, doi:10.5281/zenodo.3960218), CC0. Retrieved 2026-11-06.
- stillwater-weather/stillwater_daily_2021-2025.csv: NOAA NCEI GHCN-Daily (doi:10.7289/V5D21VHZ), station USW00003965, Stillwater Regional Airport. Public domain under NCEI's Open Data Policy (PD 10-2-02); the dataset pages themselves carry no license text. Retrieved 2026-11-06.
- oklahoma-wheat/ok_county_winter_wheat_2000-2025.csv: USDA NASS Quick Stats bulk file qs.crops_20261105, Oklahoma county winter wheat. Public domain (NASS Citation Request page), with a request to acknowledge USDA-NASS. Retrieved 2026-11-06.

## Stop 3 papers (corpus/papers/)

## Stop 4 charts (04_see/charts/)

Two drawn from real data (NOAA Mauna Loa and global CO2; palmerpenguins, CC0) and four from synthetic data labeled as such on the chart; see 04_see/charts/SOURCES.md.
