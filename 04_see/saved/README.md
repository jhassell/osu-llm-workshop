# Saved chart extractions

What `python 04_see/see.py <chart> --extract` and `python 04_see/compare.py` produced for the six shipped charts on November 6, 2026, with NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL: for each chart, the extracted CSV, the model's whole reply (`.reply.txt`), the comparison image (`-compare.png`) and the error table (`-errors.csv`).

Use them when the vision model is slow or down: open `penguin_bars-compare.png` and `dense_overlap-compare.png` and read their error tables, which is the whole of Stop 4's checkpoint. Your own chart needs the live model.
